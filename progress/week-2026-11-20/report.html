4. Tight Bounds for Differentially Private Cut Release
arXiv · 14 Nov 2026

A polynomial-time algorithm releases a nonnegative weighted synthetic graph under (ε,δ)-edge differential privacy. For any cut, the additive error is O((n log n)/(ε γ)) with high probability, improving prior O(n log^7 n) to O(n log n) for constant parameters, and a matching Ω(n log n) lower bound is proved.

Why it matters for Well7: Improves cut error bound to O(n log n) with matching lower bound for private synthetic graphs.
